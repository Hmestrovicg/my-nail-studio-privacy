NineDudeGames · My Nail Studio

Privacy Policy

Effective and last updated: 12 November 2026

This policy explains how NineDudeGames ("we", "us") handles information in My Nail Studio, our Android game (package com.ndg.mynailstudio), and when you contact us or visit this policy page.

Privacy contact: theninedude@gmail.com.

Current release: Android 0.1.0. Your campaign and settings stay on your device. There is no game-account registration or cloud-save service. Production ad requests and paid purchases are disabled in this release.

Upcoming 0.1.1 update: a neutral age-group question stores only an age band on your device, with a "Prefer not to say" option. The ad-consent, advertising and purchase flows stay inactive for children, teens and unanswered ages. Only users who select 18 or older are eligible for these flows. Production ads and purchases remain disabled in this update.

The game includes Google advertising and billing components. Their presence does not mean that paid purchases or ad requests are enabled. We will update this policy before introducing materially different data handling.

1. Information stored by the game

My Nail Studio stores your progress, nail designs, studio name, in-game inventory, story choices, achievements, tutorial progress and preferences in the app's storage on your device. These records let you continue playing and keep your chosen controls and appearance.

The game does not upload those records to NineDudeGames. Its customers, posts, followers and competitions are simulated game features; they do not connect to a real social network or publish your designs online.

We do not require your real name, email address or a separate game account to play. We have not added a separate developer-operated analytics or automatic crash-reporting service. Diagnostic logs may be stored locally; if you choose to send us a report, the information you send is handled as described below.

2. Google services, advertising and purchases

Google Play

Google independently handles your Play account, app distribution and platform services under the Google Privacy Policy. Google Play may make aggregated installation, device and app-quality reports available to developers. These are separate from uploading your campaign save to us.

Advertising

The app integrates Google AdMob and Google's User Messaging Platform for advertising and consent choices. In the current 0.1.0 release, production ad requests are disabled.

When advertising is enabled in a version of the app, Google's advertising services may process an IP address (including approximate location inferred from it), advertising or app identifiers, ad interactions, and technical or diagnostic information. These are used to provide and measure ads, maintain the service and prevent fraud. See How Google uses information for advertising and Google's Mobile Ads SDK data disclosures.

Where required, the app presents Google's consent choices before requesting ads. In versions where advertising privacy options are available, you can revisit them through Ad privacy choices in the game's settings. Android also provides advertising-privacy controls in device settings. Availability and wording vary by device and region.

Purchases

Paid purchases and restore are unavailable in the current release. It does not send purchase tokens to a NineDudeGames verification server. Cosmetic previews do not make a purchase. Before enabling purchases or a verification service, we will update this policy to describe the actual transaction information, recipients and retention. We do not receive your payment-card details from Google Play.

3. Support, feedback and internal testing

If you email us, we receive your email address, the content of your message and any attachments you choose to send, such as screenshots or diagnostic logs. We use this information to respond, investigate problems and handle privacy requests. Please avoid sending passwords, payment details or unrelated personal information.

If you ask to join an internal test, we use the Google-account email address you provide to manage access through Google Play Console. Google also processes that information to administer the test. You can ask us to remove your address from our tester list.

For information we directly handle, our purposes are providing requested support or testing access, maintaining the game and complying with applicable obligations. Where data-protection law requires a legal basis, we rely on our legitimate interests in those activities, your consent where required, or applicable legal obligations.

4. Sharing and security

We do not sell your local game saves or support correspondence. Information you send us may be processed by providers needed to deliver email, manage testing or host this page. We may disclose information if required by law or necessary to protect rights and address abuse.

We take reasonable measures to protect information we control, but no device, email service or internet transmission can be guaranteed completely secure. Google and GitHub may process information outside your country under their own policies and applicable safeguards.

5. Retention and deletion

  • Local game data: remains on your device until removed. Starting a new game preserves older save files, so it is not a complete deletion. To remove all app-local saves, settings and logs on Android, use the device's app settings to clear My Nail Studio's storage. This permanently removes local progress. Separately retained copies or backups must be deleted separately.
  • Support correspondence: we retain it only as long as needed to resolve the request, maintain relevant support history or satisfy applicable legal requirements, then delete it when no longer needed.
  • Tester addresses: we retain them while needed to administer your participation, and remove them when you request removal or they are no longer needed.
  • Provider records: data controlled independently by Google or GitHub is subject to their retention policies and privacy-request processes.

We cannot remotely access or erase a save that exists only on your device. The game has no separate NineDudeGames account to delete. To request access to, correction of or deletion of information we hold, contact theninedude@gmail.com.

6. Your privacy rights

Depending on your location and the circumstances, you may have rights to access, correct, erase or receive a copy of your personal information, restrict or object to processing, and withdraw consent without affecting earlier lawful processing. You may also complain to your local data-protection authority. Contact us to exercise rights relating to information we control; we may need enough information to verify your request. For data independently controlled by a provider, use that provider's privacy controls or contact process.

7. Children's privacy

The game does not ask players to provide a real name or contact details to play. Version 0.1.0 does not ask for age. In the upcoming 0.1.1 update, an age-group question is answered locally or skipped; we do not request a date of birth, upload the answer, or turn it into an account. The answer remains on the device until app storage is cleared. Restarting or starting a new game does not change it. Children, teens and users who do not answer can play without ads, ad-consent requests or purchase prompts. The app does not initialize its advertising or billing flows for these profiles. These protections also apply to users whose age answer is missing or invalid.

We do not knowingly solicit personal information directly from children. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, a parent or guardian can contact us so we can investigate and delete information we control where appropriate. An app's content rating does not itself establish parental consent for data processing.

8. This privacy-policy website

This page is hosted on GitHub Pages. It has no analytics scripts, advertising, forms or cookies added by NineDudeGames. GitHub may process technical request information, such as your IP address and browser information, to operate and secure hosting. See the GitHub Privacy Statement. Following an external link opens a service governed by its own privacy practices.

9. Changes and contact

We will publish revisions on this page and update the date above. Where required, material changes will also be brought to your attention in the app or through an appropriate notice before the changed processing begins.

For privacy questions, requests or concerns, contact NineDudeGames at theninedude@gmail.com, with "My Nail Studio privacy" in the subject if possible.
